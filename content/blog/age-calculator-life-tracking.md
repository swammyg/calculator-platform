# Age Calculator: Track Your Life in Years, Months, Days and Zodiac

How old am I exactly? An age calculator answers with more than a whole number. It compares a birth date with today—or another reference date—to show completed years, remaining months and days. It can also make an ordinary birthday more interesting by showing total days lived and the zodiac sign associated with the date.

## Why calculate exact age?

People use an age calculator for curiosity, family-history research, school or work forms, eligibility checks and milestone planning. The key is the reference date. Someone born on 15 May is not simply “35” forever during the year: their exact age changes every month and day.

## Use the age calculator

1. Open the [Age Calculator](https://time.kalko.uk/#/age-calculator).
2. Select your birth date.
3. Optionally select a reference date for a historical calculation.
4. Read the years, months and days result, plus next-birthday countdown and zodiac sign.

For formal applications, use the rules published by the organisation requesting the information. A website calculator is useful for a check, but cannot interpret every legal or administrative definition of age.

## Fun ways to use the result

Calculate the number of days lived before a major birthday, compare generations in a family tree or set a countdown to the next milestone. A leap-day birthday is a good reminder that calendar calculations need dates, not only a division of days by 365.25.

## Zodiac signs, lightly held

The tool maps a birth date to the familiar Western zodiac sign. That can be a fun social detail, but it is not a scientific measure of personality. Treat it as a conversation starter, not a decision-making system.

## Accuracy tips

- Enter the date in the correct day/month order shown by the control.
- Use a reference date for historical age rather than trying to count backwards.
- Check leap-year and end-of-month dates carefully.
- Keep official identity documents as the source of truth for formal matters.

## FAQ

### How accurate is it?

For valid dates, it calculates calendar age from the selected reference date. Time of birth and local rules can matter in exceptional official cases.

### What is my zodiac sign?

Enter your birth date; the result panel shows the conventional sign.

### Why does exact age matter?

It is useful for records, eligibility checks, research and personal milestones.
